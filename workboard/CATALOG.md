## agent: Human body and movement

**Owns:** hydration, heat strain, energy, walking speed, inhalation, percepts

**Must not own:** choosing actions, which belongs to the minds

**Still wanted:**
- Evacuation route timings
- ISO 7933 reference cases
- Sleep deprivation evidence, to replace the inherited exhaustion rule

## atmos: Everyday weather and wind

**Owns:** wind column by pressure level, rain, temperature, humidity

**Must not own:** ash, storms as events

**Still wanted:**
- ERA5 pressure levels before 2021 (needs a free CDS account)
- Local rain gauges around La Soufriere

## eruption: Eruption source and unrest

**Owns:** catalogues, VEI by island size, precursors, case timelines, phases, mass rate, tremor and gas signs

**Must not own:** how material travels once it leaves the vent

**Still wanted:**
- Erupted mass by phase for St Vincent 2021
- Column heights by event

## exposure: Shelter, materials and lethality

**Owns:** shelter materials, ash roof load, dynamic pressure fragility, burns

**Must not own:** needs and physiology

**Still wanted:**
- Building damage survey for St Vincent 2021
- Ash load collapse thresholds

## gas: Volcanic gas

**Owns:** surface SO2 and its exposure

**Must not own:** source flux, which comes from eruption

**Still wanted:**
- Ground SO2 readings
- Satellite SO2 mass by day

## impact: Fatalities and evacuation records

**Owns:** fatalities by hazard and distance, evacuation counts, damage records, used as validation targets

**Must not own:** any model. These are targets, never inputs

**Still wanted:**
- Brown et al. 2017 fatalities database
- EM-DAT records (needs registration)

## lahar: Lahars

**Owns:** channel flow depth and arrival, rain triggers

**Must not own:** rain and ash production

**Still wanted:**
- 2021 lahar chronology
- Rain-trigger thresholds

## lava: Lava and dome growth

**Owns:** dome footprint, effusion rate, flow front

**Must not own:** island-wide flows, since 2021 built a dome

**Still wanted:**
- Dome growth rate from observatory reports

## pdc: Pyroclastic density currents

**Owns:** flow arrival, runout, deposit, surge margin

**Must not own:** what triggers a collapse

**Still wanted:**
- 2021 PDC deposit polygons
- Primary paper on the first flow timing
- FlowDat or similar runout statistics

## seismic: Earthquakes

**Owns:** earthquake catalogues near volcanoes, felt reports

**Must not own:** a wave solver, for now

**Still wanted:**
- Local seismic network catalogue (UWI SRC); global catalogues miss small local swarms

## storm: Tropical storms and cyclones

**Owns:** cyclone tracks and rates, storm wind and rain as forcing

**Must not own:** everyday weather, lahar triggers

**Still wanted:**
- Local storm rainfall records
- EM-DAT storm losses (needs registration)

## tephra: Ash and tephra fall

**Owns:** column, fallout, deposit load, grain size

**Must not own:** rain-driven remobilisation

**Still wanted:**
- 2021 St Vincent isopachs and ash loads
- Barbados ash load measurements

## terrain: Land surface

**Owns:** elevation, drainage, land cover, roughness, bathymetry, static hazard zones

**Must not own:** hazards, weather
