# T-100: Collect open disaster data

**Stream:** WS1 (data)
**Depends on:** nothing

## Goal
Download open volcano, storm, tsunami, earthquake, island and weather data, write real eruption case studies with exact quotes, build real-event scenarios, and write a report comparing the engine with the data.

Never invent a value. Missing data stays empty, with the reason recorded.

## Files you may change
This work happens outside this branch for now. `T-101` moves the results into category records.

## Done means
1. The locked data checker passes, including re-fetching every quote live.
2. A separate reviewer spot-checks facts against their sources.
3. The report states what's missing and where the data contradicts the model.

## Out of scope
`contracts.py`, `engine/`, and anything needing a login, such as EM-DAT (`T-103`).
