# T-101: Move collected data into category records

**Stream:** WS1 (data)
**Depends on:** T-001

## Goal
Turn the data loop's manifest into datasets.toml records per category, with a real sha256 and a content check. Licence stays unconfirmed until read from the provider.

## Files you may change
`stranded_physics/domains/*/datasets.toml`

Anything else needs a note in `workboard/changes/`.

## Done means
1. Targets and tolerances in `gates.toml` were committed before running.
2. `python -m stranded_physics.catalog` succeeds.
3. Every new value is a `Parameter` with a source, or marked `assumption`.
4. Someone other than the author re-ran it.

## Out of scope
`contracts.py`, `engine/`, other categories' folders, and any quantum or classical mind logic.
