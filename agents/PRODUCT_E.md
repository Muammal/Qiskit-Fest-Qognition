**Code Ownership**: `pitch/`, `demo-pack/` (shared with Tech)

---

## Deliverables & Milestones

### 1. Narrative Framing & Banned Language Rules
Ensure all presentation materials and copy follow rigorous scientific communication standards:
- **Banned Claims**: Never state that the quantum computer "thinks like a human," provides an "exponential speedup for survival," or proves "the human brain is quantum."
- **Approved Framing**: The quantum circuit *models* non-commuting cognitive order effects using quantum probability math; physiological stress is simulated via real physical qubit decoherence on IBM Quantum processors.

1. **Hook (0:00 - 0:30)**: "Why do humans make irrational, paradoxical decisions under survival stress?"
2. **The Innovation (0:30 - 1:15)**: The 2-qubit cognitive model, non-commuting order effects, and physical decoherence on IBM hardware.
3. **Live Demonstration (1:15 - 2:15)**: Walkthrough of the island simulation and the "Why Did He Do That?" inspector.
4. **Empirical Results (2:15 - 2:45)**: Quantum vs. classical divergence curves across batch runs.
5. **Conclusion (2:45 - 3:00)**: Real-world applications of quantum cognitive modeling.
