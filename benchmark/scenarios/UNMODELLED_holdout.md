# Holdout events outside the ship model

Event roster and cause labels: Elinder and Erixson 2012, PNAS 109(33):13220, Table 1. The exclusions concern missing mechanisms, not outcomes; a breach proxy would omit the defining hazard.

- `vestris_1928`: Weather-driven listing and capsizing require stability and storm dynamics absent from the breach model.
- `morro_castle_1934`: Fire requires heat, smoke and evacuation hazards; breach flooding cannot represent it.
- `princess_victoria_1953`: Storm-driven flooding and capsizing require wave ingress and transverse stability.
- `estonia_1994`: Ro-ro deck flooding and capsizing require free-surface and transverse stability dynamics.
- `princess_of_the_stars_2008`: Storm capsizing requires wave and stability dynamics absent from the model.
- `bulgaria_2011`: Weather-driven listing and capsizing require stability dynamics, not just a fixed hull breach.
