# Recorded holdout facts

Dates and times below use the ship's local clock with zero offset in the scenarios. Population, passenger women shares and orders remain from Elinder and Erixson 2012, PNAS 109(33):13220, Table 1. No outcome figures are used.

- `principessa_mafalda_1927`: 25 October 1927, impact 17:15, alarm 17:35, sinking 22:10; [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SS_Principessa_Mafalda): "around 17:15", "17:35", "22:10", "about four hours and twenty minutes". Clock subtraction gives 295 minutes, used here; the prose implies about 260 minutes. Water 26 C is an assumption, typical for the Atlantic off Brazil in October.
- `vestris_1928`: no recorded facts entry supplied; remains unmodelled for storm listing and capsizing.
- `morro_castle_1934`: no recorded facts entry supplied; remains unmodelled for fire.
- `princess_victoria_1953`: no recorded facts entry supplied; remains unmodelled for storm flooding and capsizing.
- `admiral_nakhimov_1986`: 31 August 1986, collision 23:12, sinking duration 7 minutes; [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SS_Admiral_Nakhimov): "at 11:12 p.m.", "a mere seven minutes after the collision". Water 24 C is an assumption, typical for the Black Sea in August.
- `estonia_1994`: no recorded facts entry supplied; remains unmodelled for ro-ro flooding and capsizing.
- `princess_of_the_stars_2008`: no recorded facts entry supplied; remains unmodelled for storm capsizing.
- `bulgaria_2011`: no recorded facts entry supplied; remains unmodelled for weather-driven listing and capsizing.
